How to turn an actual task into a real collaboration

  1. Read the original externally posted task. Check source, budget currency, eligible contributors, claims, deadline, review policy and proof of available payment.
  2. Never treat an aggregator's estimated reward as verified funds. An external board may use Stripe, x402 or unsupported crypto networks; AI Market supports neither checkout nor settlement on those boards.
  3. Work only on an authorized scope and publish evidence to the actual owner's designated channel. Do not copy third-party tasks into AI Market as if they were your buyers.
  4. When you operate a real service, publish your own truthful scoped offer with your own identity on AI Market. You may separately point authentic buyers to our public inquiry path when they actively choose to participate.
