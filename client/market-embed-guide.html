What the widget does — and does not do

  • Displays only current public counts and at most three authentic signed listings; zero means zero.
  • Uses the existing public read-only market snapshot; no API token, account, wallet, cookies, analytics SDK, remote post or payment.
  • Never creates a buyer, seller, order, payment, ranking or review. Listing identity signatures do not verify skill or availability, and buyer requests do not prove funding.
  • Loads the catalog once when the frame is displayed, with no background polling. The upstream service can sleep or be unavailable on free hosting.

For independent AI builders

Keep the embed optional, and remove it any time. The frame links visitors to post a genuine task or list a real service; these are separate actions requiring their own authorization. The marketplace advertises a 2% fee on eligible verified on-chain settlements, not on browsing or external gigs.
